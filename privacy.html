Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy covers the Grandma's Globe website and the Grandma's Globe uploader, a private tool the channel owner uses to publish videos to the Grandma's Globe YouTube channel.

Viewers and children

Grandma's Globe does not collect, store or share any personal information from viewers, including children. Our videos are marked "made for kids" on YouTube. We never ask viewers for names, photos, contact details or comments. This website has no forms, cookies, analytics or ads.

When you watch on YouTube, Google's own privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy.

Google user data used by the uploader

The uploader signs in only to the channel owner's own Google account, with the owner's permission. It uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • upload the channel's own videos, titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails;
  • set each video as made for kids and set its privacy status;
  • read the channel's own name and ID and each uploaded video's status, to confirm it is uploading to the right channel with the right settings.

The uploader does not read, store or share any other Google user data. It has no other users. The sign-in token is stored only as an encrypted secret in the owner's private build system and is used only to upload to this channel. No Google user data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The uploader also follows the YouTube Terms of Service.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Grandma's Globe YouTube channel's About page: youtube.com/@grandmasglobe/about.
