Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy covers the Grandma's Globe website and the Grandma's Globe uploader, a private tool the channel owner uses to publish videos to the Grandma's Globe YouTube channel.

Viewers and children

Grandma's Globe does not collect, store or share any personal information from viewers, including children. Our videos are marked "made for kids" on YouTube. We never ask viewers for names, photos, contact details or comments. This website has no forms, cookies, analytics or ads.

When you watch on YouTube, Google's own privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy.

Use of YouTube API Services

The Grandma's Globe uploader uses YouTube API Services to publish videos to the Grandma's Globe YouTube channel. By using the uploader, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What Google user data the uploader accesses

The uploader is used only by the channel owner, and only with the owner's Google account and permission (OAuth 2.0). Through the YouTube Data API it:

  • uploads the channel's own videos, titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails;
  • marks each video as made for kids and sets its privacy status;
  • reads the channel's own name and ID, and each uploaded video's status, to confirm it is uploading to the right channel with the right settings.

It does not access any other account, any viewer's data, comments, subscribers or analytics.

How the data is used, stored and shared

  • Use: only to upload and configure the owner's own videos.
  • Storage: the only stored item is an OAuth refresh token, kept as an encrypted secret in the owner's private build system. Video titles and IDs are kept in a private log so topics aren't repeated. No other YouTube or Google data is stored.
  • Sharing: none. No Google user data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Cookies and tracking

This website and the uploader set no cookies, and use no analytics, trackers or ads.

Revoking access

You can revoke the uploader's access to your Google account at any time from the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

Data deletion

  • When access is revoked, the stored refresh token stops working immediately, and it is deleted from the build system within 7 days.
  • You can ask for any stored data about you to be deleted by contacting us (below). We delete it within 7 days of the request. The only data held is the refresh token and the list of uploaded video titles and IDs.
  • Videos on YouTube can be deleted at any time in YouTube Studio. YouTube's own retention is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Grandma's Globe YouTube channel's About page: youtube.com/@grandmasglobe/about.
